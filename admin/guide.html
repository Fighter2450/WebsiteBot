🚀 How to Go Live

Buy a domain, deploy your website, and connect everything in under 30 minutes. No tech skills needed.

This guide walks you through every step to get your website live on the internet with a professional domain name. The total cost is about $10–$15/year for the domain. Hosting is free on Netlify.

Domain: ~$12/year
Hosting: Free (Netlify)
Time: ~20 minutes
1

Download your website file

Go back to your customization page and click "Download HTML". This saves a single file to your computer — that's your entire website.

The file will be named something like my-business-website.html. Save it somewhere you can find it easily, like your Desktop.
2

Deploy to Netlify (free hosting)

Netlify hosts your website for free and gives you a live URL in under 60 seconds.

1
Go to netlify.com and click "Sign up" — use your Google or email account
2
Once logged in, look for the big box that says "Deploy manually" or "Sites"
3
Drag and drop your downloaded HTML file directly into that box
4
Netlify will give you a URL like quirky-name-123456.netlify.app — your site is already live!
→ Go to Netlify
Netlify's free plan is more than enough for a small business website. You never need to upgrade.
3

Buy your domain name

A domain like www.mybusiness.com costs about $10–$15 per year. We recommend Namecheap — it's the cheapest and easiest.

1
Go to namecheap.com and type your business name in the search box
2
Pick a .com domain if available. It usually costs $8–$14/year
3
Add it to your cart, create an account, and check out. Turn off any add-ons you don't need
→ Go to Namecheap
If your exact .com name is taken, try adding your city — like mikes-pizza-houston.com. It still looks professional.
4

Connect your domain to Netlify

This is the only slightly technical step — but just follow these instructions and you'll be done in 5 minutes.

In Netlify:

1
Go to your site in Netlify → click Site configuration → Domain management
2
Click "Add a domain" and type the domain you just bought (e.g. mybusiness.com)
3
Netlify will show you two nameserver addresses — copy them (they look like dns1.p01.nsone.net)

In Namecheap:

4
Go to namecheap.com → Domain List → click Manage next to your domain
5
Under Nameservers, change the dropdown from "Namecheap BasicDNS" to "Custom DNS"
6
Paste the two nameserver addresses from Netlify into the boxes and click the green checkmark to save
DNS changes take 15 minutes to 24 hours to go live worldwide. This is normal — just wait and your domain will work on its own.
Once connected, Netlify automatically sets up HTTPS/SSL (the padlock in the browser) for free.
5

You're live! 🎉

Once DNS propagates, type your domain into any browser and you'll see your website. Share it on Google, Yelp, Facebook, and your Google Business profile.

To get found on Google, go to Google Business Profile and add your new website there too — it helps customers find you in local searches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code? ▾
No. Everything in this guide is point-and-click. The only "technical" step is copy-pasting two nameserver addresses from one website to another.
What if my domain name is taken? ▾
Try adding your city name (e.g. myrestaurant-dallas.com), or use .net or .co instead of .com. These are still professional and often cheaper.
How long does it take for my site to go live? ▾
Your site is live on Netlify instantly. Connecting your custom domain takes between 15 minutes and 24 hours depending on your internet provider.
Is Netlify actually free? ▾
Yes. Netlify's free plan supports up to 100GB of traffic per month — plenty for any small business website. You only pay for the domain (about $12/year from Namecheap).
Can I update the website later? ▾
Yes. Go back to your customization page anytime, make changes, download the new HTML, and drag it into Netlify again. It replaces the old version instantly.
